# 台词专项阶段契约

本阶段只拥有用户点名的场景台词修订稿，以及解释这些修订所需的简短依据。它不拥有整集结构、
场景因果、人物视觉身份、镜头、提示词或审查结论。

项目内需要更新 `剧本.md` 时，由 `$short-drama-write` 负责落盘；本阶段返回准确的替换块、保留项和
真实未决项。需要独立 verdict 时交 `$short-drama-review`。局部修订不冒充审查通过。

## 本阶段规则

### `DIA`

| ID | Class | Knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| DIA-01 | reviewed_invariant | Preserve creator-supplied facts, specified exact lines, character knowledge boundaries, scene entry/exit state, and available actions. Dialogue polish cannot invent a debt, identity, secret, evidence chain, prop, time limit, or relationship solely to create conflict. |
| DIA-02 | craft_default | At a consequential exchange, each speaker with an agenda has a current objective, relevant knowledge boundary, risk or protection, usable leverage, and a strategy that can be blocked. Transactional or atmospheric speakers stay brief and need not receive a secret or arc. |
| DIA-03 | craft_default | A kept line executes an identifiable action such as probing, pressuring, bargaining, concealing, defining a boundary, verifying, refusing, or redefining the relationship. A line that cannot be labeled and carries no necessary information is merged, moved into action, or cut. |
| DIA-04 | reviewed_invariant | Subtext has a present reason not to speak directly and an observable clue in wording, action, evidence, public scope, or known contradiction. When no such reason exists, ordinary direct speech remains valid. |
| DIA-05 | craft_default | Exposition enters through present use, cost, accusation, bargaining, verification, correction, or public consequence. Characters do not recite background that both participants already know merely to inform the audience. |
| DIA-06 | reviewed_invariant | Character voice is distinguished by what the speaker notices, what counts as proof, how they control distance, how they protect themselves, and how speech changes under pressure. Interchangeable lines that differ only by catchphrase, catch-all slang, or sentence length do not establish distinct voices. |
| DIA-07 | craft_default | At consequential turns, a listener's response or deliberate nonresponse changes the next line, action, tactic, or available choice. Repeated demands without a changed strategy or new cost are compressed or interrupted by a sourced action. |
| DIA-08 | craft_default | A dialogue action beat changes access, handles evidence or leverage, exposes a contradiction, alters public scope, supplies a fact that demands response, or records a continuity-relevant state. Decorative gestures and emotion labels are not inserted at a fixed density merely to animate the page. |
| DIA-09 | taste_option | Sentence length, interruption, silence, repetition, slang, dialect, profanity, poetry, and directness are style choices governed by character, relationship, genre, and production form. No universal word count, quip count, turn count, or attack-defense rhythm is a quality gate. |
| DIA-10 | reviewed_invariant | When the creator fixes exact wording, terminology, a number of attempts, a time boundary, or another explicit constraint, the revision keeps it exact and makes the surrounding exchange work around it. It does not satisfy the constraint by adding an unrequested device or undoing its dramatic consequence. |
| DIA-11 | craft_default | A focused revision carries a preserve set and changes only the lines, cues, and adjacent actions needed for the named problem. A project-bound result is delivered as a replacement block for the screenplay owner, who retains final file integration. |
| DIA-12 | reviewed_invariant | Revised dialogue is performable with the established setting, bodies, props, knowledge, and time. A cue describes executable delivery or action; it cannot require a new event, off-screen information, or a performance the stated production cannot carry. |

规则分级由高到低：`structural_invariant`（结构缺陷，阻断）、
`reviewed_invariant`（需证据判断）、`craft_default`（常用做法，可覆盖）、
`taste_option`（创作者选择，不作缺陷）。创作者已接受的事实优先于本表。
